# Master-thesis
## MSc. Social Protection
## University of Applied Sciences Bonn-Rhein-Sieg.

Summary of my Master's Thesis on AI experiences in the Public Sector.

* Since most of the evidence has focused on a group of economies, such as the United States, China, and Europe, this research was focused on developing countries.
* A Qualitative Multi Method Approach with Two Stages was followed:
   * Narrative Literature Review: what is known about AI in the public sector of developing countries, and how this compares with trends identified in developed economies.
   * Two Case Studies of Social Protection institutions in Latin America: The Superintendency of Social Security (SUSESO) in Chile and the National Planning Department (DNP) in Colombia.
* Theoretical Framework: the Four AI Challenges model developed by Wirtz et al. (2019), which is focused on public organizations.
   * Wirtz, B. W., & Müller, W. M. (2019). An integrated artificial intelligence framework for public management. Public Management Review, 21(7), 1076–1100. https://doi.org/10.1080/14719037.2018.1549268
